How it works

Three steps. Simplified.

1

Start a dazzle

A group of zebras is a dazzle. Create the trip, add the dates, and invite your herd.

2

Track what you paid

Snap a receipt or type it in. Split evenly, by item, or by the nights each person stayed.

3

Earn your stripes, close out

Tap Done when you're finished. When everyone has their stripes, close out opens Venmo, Cash App or PayPal with everything pre-filled.
