OUTFIT RULES
Three slots, one item each: head; face and neck; body. Outfits sit on the locked anatomy and never change its shape.
The hoodie puts its hood up: it hides whatever is in the head slot (the hat comes back when the hoodie comes off).
Face and neck share a slot, so it’s glasses or a chain, not both. The bride sash lives here too, so it can go over any top.
Slots use the same anchor points on the bust and the full zebra, so one drawing works in both.
Every item ships only after passing 14 checks: 7 moods × 2 forms.
Lenses are tinted no more than 55%, so the eyes (the mood) always read.
Body items cover the chest only. The striped barrel stays visible, so Done status is never hidden.
Mood extras (z’s, sweat drop, sparkles) draw above outfits.
Palette only: ink, hide, volt, pink, blush. One exception: Jersey pack team colors (blue, green, purple, red), outfit art only. Outline weight scales up at herd size.
Nothing realistic or branded: no logos, flags, real teams or characters.
